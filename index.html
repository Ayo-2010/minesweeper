How to play

Clear the whole field without setting off a mine. The numbers tell you how many mines touch each square.

  1. Left click a square to reveal it.
  2. The first click is always safe, along with the squares around it.
  3. Numbers count the mines touching that square, from one to eight.
  4. Right click a square to place a flag on a suspected mine.
  5. Reveal every safe square to win the round.
  6. Clicking a mine ends the game. The face button restarts.
  7. The timer at the top right tracks how long the round takes.
